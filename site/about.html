About

One person. One room. One agent that learns.

PoorMad started as a refusal — that capable AI had to be closed, centralized, and extractive. Most AI is rented: you type into someone else's machine, someone else's memory, someone else's rules. PoorMad is the opposite. It lives on your side of the wall — learning, remembering, and improving on your terms.

Vision

An intelligence that belongs to you. Most AI is rented. PoorMad lives on your side of the wall — learning, remembering, improving on your terms. A future where anyone can run an agent that compounds, rather than resetting every session.

Mission

Ship a real agent, from a laptop to a fleet. Make a genuinely self-improving agent anyone can run, extend, and trust. Install it, point it at a model, and it begins to learn. No account required. No lock-in.

Cause

Sovereignty over your own intelligence. Your agent's memory, skills, and judgment should be yours — not a product someone can switch off or read. Privacy by default. Local-first. Open source. Built to be unique and yours only.

Research

The learning loop. How does an agent get permanently better from use, without a central model swallowing everyone's data? A tight loop of skills (experience → reusable, sharpened), memory (semantic recall across sessions), and orchestration (tools, subagents, scheduled jobs) — running locally.
