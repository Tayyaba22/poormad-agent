About

Our mission

We build a self-improving AI agent that learns from experience and stays under your control. PoorMad is local-first, open source, and yours only.

What we believe

  • Local-first — memory and skills live in your PoorMad home. Privacy by default.
  • Developer-centric — docs and a typed gateway SDK make any surface easy to connect.
  • Open by design — MIT licensed, built in the open, capability compounds locally.
  • Yours only — one person, one room. No surveillance, no lock-in.

Building tomorrow

Join us in exploring what a personal, self-improving agent can do — and creating a better future for how people work with AI.
