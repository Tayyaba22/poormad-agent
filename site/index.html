00 — Story

One person.
One room.
One agent that learns.

PoorMad started as a refusal — that capable AI had to be closed, centralized, and extractive.

Most AI is rented: you type into someone else's machine, someone else's memory, someone else's rules. PoorMad is the opposite. It lives on your side of the wall — learning, remembering, and improving on your terms. The longer you keep it, the smarter it gets.

01 — Capabilities

What it actually does.

Same brain across every surface. The agent compounds instead of resetting.

skills

Learns from experience

When it solves something non-trivial, PoorMad writes a reusable skill — then sharpens it on the next run. Capability compounds locally, in your home, not in someone's datacenter.

memory

Recall that persists

A deepening model of who you are — searchable by meaning. No re-explaining context every session.

orchestration

Tools & subagents

Scheduled jobs, delegation, tool registry. Point it at any model and let it coordinate.

surfaces

20+ platforms

CLI, desktop, web portal, messengers — one gateway, one memory. Sessions resume anywhere.

sovereignty

Local-first

Privacy by default. Open source (MIT). Built to be unique and yours only.

01 — VISION

An intelligence that belongs to you.

Most AI is rented. PoorMad lives on your side of the wall — learning, remembering, improving on your terms. A future where anyone can run an agent that compounds.

02 — MISSION

Ship a real agent. From a laptop to a fleet.

Make a genuinely self-improving agent anyone can run, extend, and trust. Install it, point it at a model, and it begins to learn. No account required. No lock-in.

03 — CAUSE

Sovereignty over your own intelligence.

Your agent's memory, skills, judgment should be yours — not a product someone can switch off or read. Privacy by default. Local-first. Open source. Yours only.

04 — RESEARCH

The learning loop.

How does an agent get permanently better from use, without a central model swallowing everyone's data? A tight loop of skills, memory, orchestration — running locally.
